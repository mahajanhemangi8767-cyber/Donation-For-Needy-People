Short Stories of Helping Needy People

Helping a Family in Need

Last year, we received a donation request from a family struggling with medical expenses. After raising funds, we were able to pay for their surgery, which ultimately saved their child's life. This family is now actively volunteering and helping others in their community.

Feeding the Homeless

One of our most impactful donation campaigns focused on feeding homeless people during the winter. With your contributions, we provided hot meals to over 200 people in need. Many expressed their gratitude and shared stories of how small acts of kindness had restored their faith in humanity.

Research on Helping Needy People

Impact of Volunteering on Mental Health

Studies show that volunteering can significantly boost your mental health. A study by the Harvard Medical School found that people who volunteer are less likely to experience depression and anxiety. By helping others, you also help yourself!

The Power of Small Donations

Research from the University of British Columbia has shown that small, consistent donations can be just as impactful as large ones. A $5 donation can feed a child for a day, and multiple small donations can create long-term change in a community.
